## Overview
This delta wing was cut out of craft-store foamboard using my wife's napkin-drawn planform. The motor is an inrunner from an old EDF unit, spinning a 3-blade propeller from my 5" racing quadcopter. It flies on either 4S or 6S and is quite fast. The c.g. placement was a little hard to guess and turned out to be about an inch forward of ideal on its first flight test; some lead weights at the nose made the second attempt perfect. I left the servo linkages in a higher-throw config for better low-speed controllability, but ended up setting fairly low rates on my radio once I got it trimmed.
