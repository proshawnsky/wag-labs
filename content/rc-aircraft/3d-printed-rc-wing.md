## Overview
A fully 3D printed flying wing, designed with aerodynamic analysis and flown with a custom state estimator.

- Used XFLR5 for lift, drag, CG, and stability analysis
- Programmed a custom extended Kalman filter on a Pixhawk flight computer using onboard gyros
- Adaptive cubic infill with internal slots for cable routing
- Fully integrated control surface hinges and cooling ducts
- 4 successful flights
