Model aviation is a pastime I've enjoyed since being introduced to it in middle school. I've flown foam trainers, balsa gliders, warbirds, 3D planes, custom-engineered 3D printed planes, EDF jets, racing quadcopters, 500-size helicopters, and numerous foamboard scratch-builds. 

### Tools and Capabilities

- **Airframe design & build:** foamboard scratch-building, 3D-printed airframe design
- **Flight control:** flight controller firmware (Betaflight, INAV, Ardupilot, PX4), PID tuning, GPS / autonomous flight modes, QGroundControl, Mission Planner, MAVLink
