I'm an aerospace engineer devoted to challenging myself through making things.

In my professional work, I've developed flight software and guidance algorithms for both spacecraft and unmanned jet aircraft, high-stakes applications where small errors are consequential.

Away from work, I build machines, design control systems, turn rough lumber into furniture, and fly everything from foam gliders to full size airplanes.

The subjects look unrelated, but an endeavor in each one follows the same arc. It starts with a steep learning curve, moves through a long middle of failures that demand persistence, and ends with something I can fly, use, or display proudly. I chase that arc, the lessons learned along the way, and the satisfying conclusions waiting at the end.
