## Overview
A carbon-fiber FPV racing quadcopter built and tuned from the ground up, later upgraded with GPS and autonomous flight capabilities.

- 4S LiPo, Betaflight flight controller, carbon fiber frame, 4-inch 3-blade propellers
- Tuned PID gains, D-term filtering, and calibrated ESCs
- Upgraded with a GPS receiver and ArduPilot for real-time telemetry, waypoint missions, and return-to-home capability
