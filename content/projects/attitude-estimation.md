## Overview
A Multiplicative Extended Kalman Filter (MEKF) implemented in Simulink that estimates spacecraft attitude from vector measurements.

- Implemented in Simulink as a Multiplicative Extended Kalman Filter
- Estimates the attitude quaternion and body rates from the direction of the sun and the magnetic field
