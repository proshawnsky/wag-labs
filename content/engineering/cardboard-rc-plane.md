## Overview
A no-plan, on-hand-parts RC plane built from a dumpster Amazon box — and the best flying plane I've ever made.

- Construction took 2 hours using on-hand parts and an Amazon box from the dumpster
- No design plan or analysis of any kind performed
- Thrust-to-weight ratio of ~2:1, capable of rapid vertical takeoff
- Oversized control surfaces enable superb aerobatic capabilities
