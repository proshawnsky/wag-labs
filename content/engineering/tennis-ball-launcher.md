## Overview
An automatic tennis ball launcher for my dog, built with fault-detection logic to keep it (and the dog) safe.

- Fault detection logic for motor stalls, power spikes, and paw incursions
- Persistence checks and limits on the ball sensor and motor on-time
- Coded in C++ and deployed on an Arduino
- Fully 3D printed unibody design
