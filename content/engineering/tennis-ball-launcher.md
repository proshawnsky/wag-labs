## Overview
An automatic tennis ball launcher for my dog

- Persistence checks and limits on the ball sensor and motor on-time
- Coded in C++ and deployed on an Arduino
- Fully 3D printed unibody design
- Recycled motors and ESCs from a quadcopter
- Motors spool up independently and with ramp profiles to avoid voltage sag on a small power supply
